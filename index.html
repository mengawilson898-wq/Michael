About

A dedicated partner for authors who deserve to be found.

Michael Reeves works as a book discoverability strategist and public relations partner for authors — helping self-published and bestselling writers alike strengthen how their books show up across Amazon, Goodreads, and the wider media landscape.

The approach is never one-size-fits-all. Every book is researched on its own terms — its genre, its themes, its ideal reader — before a single strategy is built around it. That care shows up in retail metadata, in review and media placement, and in how a book is presented to the audience most likely to love it.
